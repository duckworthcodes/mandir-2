Our Story

Bridging Devotion
Across Continents

MahaPoojan was born from a simple yet profound belief: that distance should never separate a devotee from the divine. Today, we are the spiritual home for millions of Hindus worldwide — a bridge of faith, culture, and sacred rituals.

🕉️ ✦ ॐ ✦ 🕉️
Our Dharma

To Make Every Hindu Feel Spiritually Present — No Matter the Miles

We partner with 100+ sacred temples across India — from the ghats of Varanasi to the hills of Tirumala — to bring live darshan, authentic pujas, and prasad to your doorstep. With over 30 million devotees in 30+ countries, MahaPoojan is the largest and most trusted devotional ecosystem.

Our technology is guided by tradition. Every ritual is performed by temple priests according to Vedic standards, and we provide video proof, real-time updates, and sanctified prasad shipped globally.
